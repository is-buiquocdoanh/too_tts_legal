Privacy Policy

App: auto post video (VieNeu Studio) · Last updated: November 1, 2026 · Contact: doanh762003@gmail.com

auto post video is a personal desktop tool that runs entirely on the owner's own computer. It helps the owner create short Vietnamese videos and publish them to the owner's own TikTok account and Facebook Page. There is no server operated by us and no other users.

1. Data we access

  • TikTok: when you log in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and your basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) via the scope user.info.basic, and uses video.upload / video.publish only to upload videos that you created and chose to post. It also reads your current posting options (privacy levels, interaction settings) from the Creator Info API before each post.
  • Videos and captions that you create in the app.

2. How data is stored

All data — access tokens, settings, videos and captions — is stored only on your computer (in the app's local .local_private and data folders). We do not operate any server, and we do not collect, sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

3. How data is used

Your TikTok data is used solely to show which account is connected and to upload or publish the videos you explicitly approve. The app never posts without your confirmation for Direct Post, never reads your followers, messages or other content, and never uses your data for advertising or analytics.

4. Third parties

Data is sent only to the official APIs needed for the features you use: TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API) and Meta Graph API for your Facebook Page. Text prompts for writing scripts may be sent to the AI provider you choose (e.g. Anthropic Claude); no TikTok account data is included in those prompts.

5. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect TikTok at any time by deleting the token in the app or revoking access in TikTok settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder removes all stored data.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

7. Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
